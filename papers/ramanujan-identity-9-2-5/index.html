The constants K and λ in Ramanujan's identity (9.2.5) of the Lost Notebook, with a hexagonal lattice sum calculator

Otakhon U. Kenjaev · Independent researcher, Khorezm, Uzbekistan · 27.09.2026

PDF · doi:10.5281/zenodo.22987480 · calculator & code

Abstract

Andrews and Berndt (Ramanujan's Lost Notebook, Part IV, pp. 215-216) could not identify the constants K and λ in Ramanujan's identity (9.2.5). We show that it holds for Re s > 2 with K = μ − ν and λ = μ + ν (sum over coprime μ, ν ≥ 1), prove it via the six-fold symmetry of the Eisenstein integers, and give an in-browser calculator for the resulting hexagonal sector lattice sum.
